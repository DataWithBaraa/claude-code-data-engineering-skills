Grain rules

One order has one or more lines. Count orders with count(DISTINCT order_id).
One account has many orders. Count customers with count(DISTINCT customer_id), and leave out unknown.
A product has one row in dim_products. Its price and cost have many versions in silver_core; the table already took the one valid on the order day.

6Whom to ask

For a new consumer: an analyst, a team lead, a new member of Finance. Read this once, run the 3 queries, and you have the numbers of the Monday trading pack: net revenue, orders and margin by month, market and category.

1What it is5 min

One row per order line of an order that was not cancelled, with its day, customer and product beside it. 12,418,906 rows, from 2022-01-01 to 2026-08-31. It lives in the warehouse as gold_marts.obt_order_lines, and it is rebuilt by every run that succeeds, daily from 05:30 UTC.

2What to trust10 min

Sum the money, never the counts. sum(net_eur) is Finance's net revenue for any selection. Orders and customers are count(DISTINCT ...), recomputed for each selection.
Cancelled orders are already out. Do not filter on status; there is none.
Euros without VAT. No shipping, no refunds. Net revenue is not what the customer paid at checkout.
The day is the UTC day of the checkout. An order placed at 01:30 in Berlin belongs to the day before.
Guests are unknown. They have no account, so they share one customer_id.
The data is as good as the last run that succeeded. When a delivery is late, the gold tasks are skipped and consumers read the gold of the last run that built it. A wrong file or row is quarantined and the rest of the day is built; the Quality tab lists both.

3Open it5 min

Read-only, and never while the daily run is writing. In Python:

import duckdb
con = duckdb.connect("data/warehouse/newvolt.duckdb", read_only=True)
con.sql("SELECT count(*) FROM gold_marts.obt_order_lines").show()

If it says the file is locked, a run is writing. Wait until the run has finished, about 12 min after 05:30 UTC. More in knowledge/connections.md.

5When something looks wrong10 min

Then the Quality tab, or the Runs page. When the gold tasks of a run were skipped, the newest day is not in.
Then write to the data engineer, with your query and the number you expected. Your correction goes into corrections.md.

Rules for every measure

A zero denominator gives a blank, never a percentage. nullif(..., 0) in every ratio.
Never add counts. In 2026-08 the orders of the 6 markets add to 97,030 only because an order ships to 1 country; the orders of the 5 categories add to 118,276, because an order with a cable and a laptop counts in both.
The day is the UTC day of the checkout. ordered_on and order_month come from it. Refund postings and cash dates are other time bases and are not in this table.
Year on year compares the same days. A new product with no sales a year before has no growth percentage.

Subject: obt_order_lines loses city and postal_code on 2026-10-07

From release 1.1.0 on 2026-10-07, gold_marts.obt_order_lines no longer carries city and postal_code. The one big table is read by 5 roles, and personal detail belongs where few people read it. None of the queries of 2026-08 read them. A query outside the log keeps working when it joins gold_marts.dim_customers, as below. Nothing else in the table changes.

-- Before, until 1.0.0
SELECT postal_code, sum(net_eur) AS net_revenue_eur
FROM gold_marts.obt_order_lines
WHERE order_month = '2026-08'
GROUP BY postal_code;

-- After, from 1.1.0: the postal code comes from the customer dimension
SELECT customers.postal_code, sum(lines.net_eur) AS net_revenue_eur
FROM gold_marts.obt_order_lines AS lines
JOIN gold_marts.dim_customers AS customers
  ON customers.customer_id = lines.customer_id
WHERE lines.order_month = '2026-08'
GROUP BY customers.postal_code;
